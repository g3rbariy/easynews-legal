Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

EasyNews Clip Factory is a local desktop tool. This policy explains how the tool handles information when its owner connects a publication account.

Data processed locally

Video files, clip settings, and publication text are processed on the owner’s computer. The tool does not operate a public user database.

Connected accounts

When the owner authorizes YouTube, TikTok, or another platform, authorization credentials are stored locally in encrypted form. The tool uses them only to perform actions the owner explicitly starts.

Sharing

Video and publication metadata are sent to a selected platform only after the owner reviews the publication and confirms it. The applicable platform then processes the data under its own policies.
